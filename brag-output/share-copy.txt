HelpDesk Pro: a Django IT support portal where every ticket runs on an SLA clock (High 4h, Moderate 24h, Low 72h), with a live operations dashboard and a 4-page Power BI report fed by the app's own key-authenticated REST API. In the sample data that's 62.5% SLA compliance, and the report shows exactly which 12 tickets breached. 30 seconds, real app, real data: github.com/VatsSanghvi/HelpDesk-Pro
